Common questions.

Is this medical advice?

No. This is educational guidance to help you observe patterns and prepare better conversations with your veterinarian.

Are directory providers verified?

Not yet. Public listings should not appear as verified until source labels and human review are in place.

Does this guide include product recommendations?

No. This platform is for education, care planning, and finding appropriate support. Product information, if ever relevant, belongs somewhere separate.

How long is the webinar?

The monthly virtual session is one hour, with practical guidance and time for Q&A.
