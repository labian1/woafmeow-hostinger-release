Behavior Medicine Service

A specialty behavior service for anxiety, fears, phobias, aggression and age-related behavior changes in companion animals, including senior-dog changes that need medical context.

When this may be a fit

Use this when nighttime anxiety, fear, aggression, confusion or other behavior change needs a specialist conversation after urgent medical causes have been considered.

Details published by the source

  • Cornell says a primary-veterinarian referral is not required for this service.
  • An online behavior history form is required before scheduling.
  • The clinic asks owners to arrange medical records, recent test results and vaccination records before the visit.
