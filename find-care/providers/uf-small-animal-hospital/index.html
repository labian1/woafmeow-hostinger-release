UF Small Animal Hospital

A university small-animal hospital offering 24-hour emergency and critical care, ICU support, advanced diagnostics and specialty services for dogs and other companion animals.

When this may be a fit

Use this for a serious emergency in the Gainesville area or for a specialty referral involving oncology, internal medicine, dentistry, cardiology or complex surgery.

Published team detail

Details published by the source

  • UF states that its emergency service is available 24/7, including holidays.
  • The hospital says emergencies can be walk-ins and referrals; some specialty services have referral requirements.
  • For emergency referrals, UF asks callers to confirm capacity and the fastest appropriate option.
