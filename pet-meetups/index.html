After three saved care moments, WoafMeow matches you one-to-one with a nearby pet family whose pace, schedule, and care reality fit yours. No swiping. No crowded event.

01

We learn the real routine

Three saved check-ins or lessons unlock matching. We use species, size, energy, mobility, temperament, schedule, location, and what the family hopes to find.

02

Wednesday brings one match

No swiping and no crowded feed. You receive one private introduction with the reasons the match may work and a low-pressure first-meeting plan.

03

Your feedback improves the next one

Afterward, rate comfort, pace, conversation, pet response, and whether you would meet again. Safety concerns block repeat matching.
