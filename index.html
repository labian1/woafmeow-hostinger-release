EARLIER HEALTH-CHANGE ALERTS

Get health-change alerts sooner.

Slide Smart Base under the WoafyPet Bed or another dog bed. It learns your dog’s normal routine and alerts you when a change lasts.

Rest
See when sleep becomes shorter or broken.
Night movement
Spot more wake-ups or restlessness.
Breathing rate
Catch a sustained rise during rest.
Heart rate
See when the resting trend shifts.
Weight
Catch ongoing gain or loss.

Get the alert. Contact your veterinarian when a change is new, concerning or worsening.
