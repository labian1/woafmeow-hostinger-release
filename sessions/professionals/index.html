Ways to take part.

Be easier to find Request a source-labeled profile that helps families understand your scope, location, and first step. Suggest a care session Bring a specific family question that deserves a clearer, evidence-aware explanation. Review a family guide Help make a practical guide more accurate, direct, and useful in a real home.

Make the information families receive usable at home.

Strong contributions start with one care moment, one concrete example, and an explicit next step. We do not publish unconfirmed speaker biographies or imply individual endorsement before participation is confirmed.

Session ideasMobility, pain, feline routine changes, emergency planning, dental decisions, nutrition, palliative care, grief, and family communication.

Directory profilesOfficial source, service area, care scope, how to contact the team, and what families should prepare first.

Family resourcesPlain-language context that supports, rather than replaces, a real veterinary relationship.

Start with a clear professional profile.

Families should be able to see when to call, what your team provides, who it is for, where you serve, and what information helps the first conversation. That is the standard we are building toward in the directory.
