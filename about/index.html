BOBBY'S STORY

Bobby was eight when joint cancer took him.

I’m Robert Luo—Bobby’s person. He was my family, my shadow and the reason WoafMeow exists.

The hardest truth is that we did not understand Bobby’s health change in time. Like so many dogs, he was very good at hiding pain. He kept following us, greeting us and being himself—until he could not anymore.

I still replay the small moments. A slower rise. A pause before moving. A night that did not look quite right. We loved him completely, but we did not yet know how much those quiet changes could mean.

I cannot give Bobby those days back. I can help another family notice sooner.— Robert Luo, Bobby’s person and WoafMeow co-founder

I built WoafMeow so a dog parent can bring one ordinary change and leave with clearer observations, safer next steps and a better conversation with the veterinarian who knows their dog.

WoafyPet comes from the same promise: support aging joints now, and quietly follow rest patterns that may help a family recognize when something has changed.

Bobby’s life was much bigger than the way he died. He was proud, gentle, funny and always close. Everything here is built from the love he gave us—and from the help I wish we had while he was still beside me.
