CAT · CARE CIRCLE LESSON

Kidney Changes Worth Noticing

Track thirst, urine, appetite, weight, and nausea without guessing at a diagnosis.

01

Build a seven-day picture

Note bowl refills, urine clump size, appetite at each meal, vomiting, body weight if available, and whether your cat seeks water from unusual places.

02

Protect eating and hydration

Offer fresh water in several quiet locations and keep food easy to reach. Do not change to a restrictive diet or add supplements without veterinary guidance.

03

Do not wait for

Seek veterinary help promptly for repeated vomiting, marked weakness, refusing food, dehydration, mouth ulcers, sudden behavior change, or difficulty urinating.

Reviewed source

This educational lesson is grounded in owner guidance from Cornell Feline Health Center. It does not diagnose a pet or replace veterinary care.
