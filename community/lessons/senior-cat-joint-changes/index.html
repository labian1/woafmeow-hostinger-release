CAT · CARE CIRCLE LESSON

Joint Changes in Older Cats

Use jumping, grooming, posture, and litter-box access as everyday clues.

01

Look beyond limping

Cats with joint pain may stop using a favorite shelf, hesitate before jumping down, groom the back less, sleep in easier-to-reach places, or miss a high-sided litter box.

02

Make access easier today

Place food, water, and a low-entry litter box on the same level. Add a stable intermediate step to a favored perch and keep nails trimmed for traction.

03

Bring useful evidence

Take a short video of one normal route, note which height is avoided, and record whether the change is new, gradual, or worse after rest.
