CAT · CARE CIRCLE LESSON

Litter Box Changes

Separate access problems from urinary or digestive urgency.

01

Describe the pattern

Note number and size of urine clumps, stool consistency, straining, vocalizing, accidents, entry posture, and whether the box location or litter recently changed.

02

Make access safer

Add a large low-entry box in a quiet, easy-to-reach location. Keep the old box during the transition and use unscented litter unless your veterinarian advises otherwise.

03

Urinary blockage is urgent

Repeated straining with little or no urine, distress, vomiting, hiding, or a painful abdomen is an emergency, especially in male cats.
