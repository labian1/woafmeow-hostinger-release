DOG & CAT · CARE CIRCLE LESSON

Restless at Night

Sort pain, toileting, thirst, breathing, and disorientation into a usable timeline.

01

Name the exact wake-up

Write the time, what happened first, where your pet went, whether they panted or vocalized, and what finally helped them settle.

02

Check the simple needs

Confirm comfortable temperature, water access, a clear toileting route, traction, and pain-free access to the sleeping area. Avoid changing several things at once.

03

Call sooner when

Breathing difficulty, repeated unproductive retching, collapse, a swollen abdomen, inability to urinate, severe distress, or sudden confusion needs urgent veterinary advice.

Reviewed source

This educational lesson is grounded in owner guidance from Merck Veterinary Manual. It does not diagnose a pet or replace veterinary care.
