Make it easier for families to understand fit.

Show the official source A public website, published location, care scope, and current way to contact your team help people make an informed first call. Describe who the care helps A useful profile explains the moment you are equipped for: mobility change, palliative support, a difficult decision, grief, or aftercare. Give families a clear first step They should be able to see whether to call, ask for a referral, bring records, or choose a different level of care before they are already overwhelmed.
